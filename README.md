# Phase Dash

> A fast-paced action game where players must shift through dimensions to overcome obstacles and survive.

## Story & Lore

**"Only the fastest can break the boundaries of reality."**

In a world where the fabric of space is collapsing, you play as a runner who discovers the ability to 'phase' between dimensions. Trapped in a high-speed temporal anomaly, you must navigate through deadly traps, shift through solid matter, and race against time to reach the core before the dimension shatters entirely. Speed is your only weapon; phasing is your only shield.
